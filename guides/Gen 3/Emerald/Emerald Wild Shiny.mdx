<Gist>Catch a wild shiny Pokemon in Emerald.</Gist>

## Before you start

This tool helps you catch wild shiny Pokemon without choosing specific IVs or
natures. For advanced RNG manipulation, including targeting IVs and natures, use
the [Emerald Wild RNG advanced tool](/emerald-wild/).

You must know your Secret ID (SID) to use this tool. If you do not know it, follow
the [Find Emerald SID guide](/gen3-sid/) first.

## Find a setup and catch your Pokemon

1. Choose a species, enter your TID and SID, and press **Find shiny setup**.
   - The tool will select the optimal setup for an easy shiny Pokémon.
   - To restrict the considered maps, actions, or leads, toggle **Ignore Recommended Setups** first.
1. Additional instructions will be displayed to catch and calibrate your Pokémon.
