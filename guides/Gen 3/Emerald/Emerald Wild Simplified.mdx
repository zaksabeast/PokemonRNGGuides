## Find a setup and catch your Pokemon

1. Choose a species, enter your TID and SID (both required, from 0 to 65535),
   and press **Find setup**. To restrict the maps, actions, or
   leads, enable **Ignore Recommended Setups** first.
2. Read the selected setup and prepare your party and required items. The tool
   selects the earliest returned setup above 50% likelihood when available;
   otherwise it selects the returned setup with the highest likelihood.
3. Follow the Catch and Calibrate instructions below the setup. Save in the
   selected location, then use the timers to soft reset and trigger the encounter.
4. Catch the Pokemon even if it is not the target. Enter its summary stats in the
   calibration form and use the highest-confidence match to update calibration.
5. Repeat the timed attempt until you catch the target. Hitting the correct
   advance can still produce a different method, so another attempt may be needed.

Changing search choices clears the current target and calibration. Timing
corrections are retained when retrying the same selected setup.
