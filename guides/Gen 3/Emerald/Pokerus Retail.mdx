## Pokérus

After each wild battle, there is a 1 / 21,845 chance that one of your Pokémon will be infected with Pokérus. While infected by Pokérus, EVs gained from battling are doubled. Pokérus is notably required to get optimal low-level Pokémon such as Level 5 Smeargle with Dragon Rage.

## Steps Overview

- **Step 1:** Determine the best setup to obtain Pokérus.
- **Step 2:** Create a Battle Video, optionally combined with Painting Reseeding, to speed up attempts.
- **Step 3:** Complete the battle and calibrate until obtaining Pokérus.

After selecting a setup, you can return directly to Step 2 or Step 3 while it remains selected. If the selected setup uses Painting Reseeding, you must complete Step 2 before continuing.

<Step step={0}>

### Find Best Setup

1. Fill the considered setup fields. Choose between stationary and wild encounters:
   - **Stationary encounter:** Kecleon and Voltorb encounters are recommended.
     - **Pros:** Pokémon generation has stable RNG advances (always 4 advances). Access to 6 Pickup Pokémon for easier calibration.
     - **Cons:** You must reset the game after each attempt. Your lead Pokémon must be high level to defeat the encounter in 1-shot.
   - **Wild encounter:**
     - **Pros:** Always available. Faster attempts.
     - **Cons:** Pokémon generation advances RNG unpredictably (between 18 and 100 advances). Hitting your target requires luck.
1. Press "Find best setup".
1. Select a setup from the results. Prefer one with a low wait, several advances resulting in Pokérus, and a high calibration chance.
1. Prepare the party shown in the selected setup. Pickup Pokémon must not be holding items.
1. Go to an encounter location with no wandering NPCs and save the game.

### Create Battle Video

Battle Video is an RNG technique that saves and restores the RNG state. It lets you wait through most of the delay once, then retry the manipulation much more quickly.

Creating a Battle Video is optional unless the selected setup uses Painting Reseeding. It requires access to the Battle Frontier after entering the Hall of Fame.

### Battle and Calibrate

1. Start the timer in the tool below.
1. When the first timer reaches 0, close the Battle Video. If no Battle Video was created, soft reset with `Start + Select + A + B` instead.
1. At the next timer, trigger Sweet Scent (wild encounter) or interact with the selected stationary Pokémon.
1. Defeat the Pokémon, then wait on the final message indicated by the timer. Don't flee because the Pickup ability won't be triggered.
1. When the final timer reaches 0, press `A` to end the battle.
1. Check every Pokémon in your party for Pokérus. If one is infected, congratulations!
1. If none of your Pickup Pokémon has an item, try again. This attempt can't be calibrated.
1. If they have items, enter the items in the fields below and press "Generate".
1. Remove held items from your Pokémon.
1. Select the update action for the closest result, then repeat this step with the adjusted calibration.
