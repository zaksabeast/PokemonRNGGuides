Without PokeReader, you can still use this guide if you know your egg seeds and TSV. See [Getting Your Egg Seeds and TSV](#getting-your-egg-seeds-and-tsv).

## Masuda Method, Shiny Charm, or Neither?

How you RNG an egg depends on whether you're using the Masuda Method (parents from games in different languages), the Shiny Charm, or both.

- **With the Masuda Method and/or Shiny Charm**, your egg seeds decide everything about the egg, including whether it's shiny. You reach the egg you want by accepting and rejecting eggs, which is what this guide covers.
- **Without either**, the egg seeds decide everything except whether it's shiny. That's decided when you accept the egg. This guide doesn't cover that yet, so use the [Egg RNG without Masuda Method or Shiny Charm guide](/retail-usum-egg-no-mmsc) for now.

## In-Game Preparations

1. Leave the parents you want to breed at the Nursery on Route 5.
2. Walk around until the Nursery lady has an egg for you.
3. Save your game in front of her before talking to her.

## Getting Your Egg Seeds and TSV

### With PokeReader

1. Open PokeReader and find your TSV in the main RNG view.
2. Go to the Daycare view to find your egg seeds, whether you have the Shiny Charm, and your parents' info.

### Without PokeReader

- To find your egg seeds without custom firmware, follow the [Find Egg Seeds guide](/retail-usum-egg-seed-no-cfw).
- With homebrew or custom firmware, back up your save with a save manager like Checkpoint and open it in PKHeX to see your egg seeds and TSV.
- Someone in the [Pokemon RNG Discord](https://www.discord.gg/d8JuAvg) might be able to help you find your TSV.

> [!NOTE]
> If you've used this tool before, click "Use as current egg seeds" after getting each egg so you don't need to find your egg seeds again.

## Find a Target

1. Enter your four egg seeds and your TSV.
2. Turn on "Masuda Method" if the parents are from games in different languages, and turn on "Shiny Charm" if you have it.
3. Choose the gender ratio of the Pokemon that will hatch. Pick "Nidoran or Volbeat/Illumise" if the egg could hatch into either Nidoran, or into Volbeat or Illumise.
4. Turn on "Same Species" if both parents are the same species.
5. Fill in each parent. Turn on "Ditto" for the parent that's a Ditto, if any. Genderless and male-only Pokemon count Ditto as the female parent.
6. Set your filters and click "Generate".
7. Click "Select" on the egg you want. Lower "Accepts + Rejects" means less work to reach it.

> [!TIP]
> Shiny eggs can take a while to reach. If you can't find one you like, increase the "Max Advances".

## Accept and Reject Eggs

1. Follow the steps below from top to bottom. Wait for a new egg each time before accepting or rejecting it.
2. Click "Done" on each step once you've finished it, so you don't lose your place.
3. If you have PokeReader, check that your egg seeds match the ones shown before each step.
4. Save your game every so often so you don't lose your progress.

> [!WARNING]
> Accepting or rejecting eggs in a different order than shown changes your egg seeds and you won't get your target. Don't accept your target egg in this step.

## Get the Egg

1. Wait for the next egg. If you have PokeReader, check your egg seeds match the ones below.
2. Accept the egg. This is your target.
3. To RNG another egg, click "Use as current egg seeds". The tool goes back to "Find a Target" with your new egg seeds already filled in.

<Gen7EggTarget />

Congrats! You should now have the egg you wanted.

## If You Did Not Get the Egg You Wanted

1. Double-check that your egg seeds were entered in the right order and that your TSV is correct.
2. Check the parents' IVs, held items, ability slots, and Ditto settings.
3. Check the "Masuda Method" setting. The parents need to be from games in different languages.
4. Check the "Shiny Charm" setting matches whether you have it.
5. Make sure you accepted and rejected the eggs in the exact order shown.
