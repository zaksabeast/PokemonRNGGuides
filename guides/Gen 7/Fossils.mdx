## In-Game Preparations

1. Have the fossil you want to restore in your bag. If you want a specific nature, put a Pokemon with Synchronize and that nature first in your party.
2. Save your game in front of the man in the Fossil Restoration Center on Route 8.
3. Talk to him, choose your fossil, and continue until he says "I turned that Fossil of yours right back into the Pokemon it used to be!" Don't press `A` yet.
4. Pause the game with `Start + Select`. In PokeReader, the NPC count should be 1.

## Find a Target

1. Enter your seed and TSV from PokeReader into the tool below.
2. Select your fossil and set "Current Advance" to the `Advances` value in PokeReader.
3. Set "Lead" to "Synchronize" with your lead's nature if the first Pokemon in your party has Synchronize. Otherwise, leave it as "Ordinary lead".
4. Set your filters and click "Generate".
5. If you can't find a fossil you like, you can increase the "Max Advances". This will increase the time to get the fossil.
6. Click "Select" on the result you want.

## Create a Timeline

1. Stay paused on the dialogue and click "Find Safe Advances" in the tool below.
2. Press `Select` to step one video frame at a time until you land exactly on one of the listed safe advances, then click it.
3. If you step past all of them, click "Show More".
4. Keep stepping and compare your advances to the ones shown. If they match, you're on the right timeline.

> [!NOTE]
> If the advances don't match, check your seed is entered correctly, then start again from the "Find a Target" step. Also check that the NPC count is 1 during this step.

## Fossil Dialogue

1. Stay paused and press `Select` to step one video frame at a time until you reach the advance below.
2. Press `A` on that advance to continue the dialogue.
3. Afterwards, press `Start + Select` to pause the game again.

> [!NOTE]
> If the advance is a while away, you can press `Start` to unpause, then pause again with `Start + Select` a little before it.

<Gen7FossilPressDialogueA />

> [!TIP]
> If the tool says your target can't be reached, stay paused and select another result in the "Find a Target" step.

</Step>

## Get the Fossil

1. Unpause. When he says "This here is xxx! You'd better take right good care of it!", press `A` right away. The timing of this press doesn't change your target.
2. Wait until the "You obtained xxx!" dialogue shows up.
3. Pause again with `Start + Select` when you're close to your target advance.
4. Press `Select` to step one video frame at a time until you reach your target advance.
5. Press `A` on your target advance.

<Gen7FossilTargetAdvance />

Congrats! You should now have the fossil Pokemon you wanted.

## If You Did Not Get the Fossil You Wanted

1. Double-check that all info has been input correctly, especially the seed and TSV.
2. Check that "Lead" matches the first Pokemon in your party.
3. Make sure you pressed `A` on the dialogue on the exact advance shown.
4. Reload your save, which gives you a new seed, and start the guide again from the first step.
5. If you're on an emulator, make sure you don't speed up the emulation before the target advance.
