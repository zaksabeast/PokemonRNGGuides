## In-Game Preparations

1. Save your game before you walk into the grass to start the cutscene.
2. Go through the cutscene and dialogue until Kukui says "Go on and choose the one you'd like to keep as your partner, cousin!" Don't press `A` yet.
3. Wait for the camera to stop moving, then pause the game with `Start + Select`. In PokeReader, the NPC count should be 1.

## Find a Target

1. Enter your seed, TSV, and TRV from PokeReader into the tool below.
2. Set "Current Advance" to the `Advances` value in PokeReader.
3. Set your filters and click "Generate".
4. If you can't find a starter you like, you can increase the "Max Advances". This will increase the time to get the starter.
5. Click "Select" on the starter you want. The results are the same for Rowlet, Litten, and Popplio, so pick any species in-game.

Shiny starters with high IVs are rare within a reasonable number of advances. You'll have better chances aiming for a max of 3 perfect IVs.

## Create a Timeline

1. Stay paused on Kukui's dialogue and click "Find Safe Advances" in the tool below.
2. Press `Select` to step one video frame at a time until you land exactly on one of the listed safe advances, then click it.
3. If you step past all of them, click "Show More".
4. Keep stepping and compare your advances to the ones shown. If they match, you're on the right timeline.

> [!NOTE]
> If the advances don't match, check your seed is entered correctly, then start again from the "Find a Target" step. Also check that the NPC count is 1 during this step.

## Kukui's Dialogue

1. Stay paused and press `Select` to step one video frame at a time until you reach the advance below.
2. Press `A` on that advance to continue Kukui's dialogue.
3. Afterwards, press `Start + Select` to pause the game again.

> [!NOTE]
> If the advance is a while away, you can press `Start` to unpause, then pause again with `Start + Select` a little before it.

<Gen7StarterPressKukuiA />

> [!TIP]
> If the tool says your target can't be reached from this timeline, start over from the "Find a Target" step.

</Step>

## Choose Starter

1. Unpause, select the starter you want, and continue until the game asks you to confirm your choice with "Yes" and "No" options.
2. Pause with `Start + Select` a little before the advance given below, then press `Select` to step one video frame at a time until you reach it.
3. Press `A` on that advance to choose "Yes".
4. Afterwards, press `Start + Select` to pause the game again.

<Gen7StarterPressYes />

</Step>

<Step step={4}>

## Get the Starter

1. Unpause and wait until the "You chose xxx!" dialogue shows up.
2. Pause again with `Start + Select` when you're close to your target advance.
3. Press `Select` to step one video frame at a time until you reach your target advance.
4. Press `A` on your target advance.

<Gen7StarterTargetAdvance />

Congrats! You should now have the starter you wanted.

## If You Did Not Get the Starter You Wanted

1. Double-check that all info has been input correctly, especially the seed and TSV.
2. Make sure you pressed `A` on Kukui's dialogue and "Yes" on the exact advances shown.
3. Reload your save, which gives you a new seed, and start the guide again from the first step.
4. If you're on an emulator, make sure you don't speed up the emulation during the last cutscene before the target advance.
