## Tools

- [A 3DS with PokeReader](/install-pokereader)
- [3DSRNGTool](https://github.com/wwwwwwzx/3DSRNGTool/releases)

## What is a Timeline?

Only use a timeline if there are one or more NPCs in your area. If you have 0 NPCs, you don't need a timeline.

NPCs use RNG advances when they blink. Each video frame, every NPC on screen may use an advance, so the RNG doesn't move forward one advance at a time. Only some advances can be landed on. The advances you can land on are your timeline.

To know which timeline you're on, you need to land on a safe advance. A safe advance is one where no NPC is blinking, so every future blink can be predicted.

## Step 1: Find Your Target

1. In 3DSRNGTool, enter your game version and TSV.
2. Input the initial seed, found in the main RNG view under `Init Seed:`.
3. If you have the Shiny Charm, check the Shiny Charm box.
4. Search for the Pokémon you want and note its advance. 3DSRNGTool calls this the "frame".

## Step 2: Get Ready in Game

1. Go through the dialogue until the next `A` press gives you the Pokémon.
2. Stay there. Any more dialogue can change your timeline.
3. In PokeReader, note your initial seed, current advance, and NPC count.

## Step 3: Find Your Timeline

1. Enter your values below and click "Find Safe Advances".
2. Pause the game with `Start + Select`, then press `Select` to step one video frame at a time.
3. Keep stepping until you land exactly on one of the listed safe advances, then click it.
4. Keep stepping and compare your advances to the ones shown.
   - If they match, you're on the right timeline.
   - If they don't match, check your initial seed and NPC count, then try again.

## Step 4: Press A on Your Target

1. If the tool says your target isn't on this timeline, pick another target in 3DSRNGTool.
2. Press `Start` to unpause, then pause again with `Start + Select` when you're close to your target.
3. Press `Select` to step one video frame at a time until you reach your target advance.
4. Press `A` on your target advance.
