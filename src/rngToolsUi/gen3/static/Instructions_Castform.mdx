### Additional Instructions for Castform

1. Fill all the PC boxes and your party with Pokémon.
1. Defeat Team Aqua in the Weather Institue. The scientist won't give you Castform right away because you don't have space.
1. Release a Pokémon in your PC.
1. Go back to the scientist to perform the RNG manipulation.
